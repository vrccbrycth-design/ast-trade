Frequently asked questions

Product FAQ

Which mango variety is offered and for what uses?
We offer Kent mango from Benin, a variety recognised for its melt-in-the-mouth flesh and strong transport performance. It suits fresh-fruit channels, importers, wholesalers and distributors looking for a mango well adapted to export.
Which sizes and packaging formats are available?
As an indication, sizes break down into A (≥650 g), B (450–650 g), C (350–450 g) and D (250–350 g). Packaging is 4 kg or 6 kg cartons with individual trays. Final selection is confirmed according to the lot, the transport mode and your specifications.
What is the seasonality of Kent mango from Benin?
Availability depends on the harvest calendar and the pace of picking. For a buying programme or a one-off operation, share your preferred shipping window so we can confirm mobilisable volumes and planning.
What are the transport conditions and shelf life?
As an indication, transport runs at 10–12 °C, for a shelf life of 10–14 days depending on the ripeness stage. Logistics conditions and the Incoterm used are validated case by case before booking.
Which export documents and contractual terms apply?
Depending on the operation, export preparation may include a commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and phytosanitary certificate. Documents, controls and contractual terms are agreed before shipment based on the customer's requirements and the country of entry.
